# Layering

## At the limit

Each layer speaks the one beneath it and knows nothing of those above.
Everything on the wire belongs to exactly one layer, whose vocabulary it
is: the lowest layer acts on what is its own and forwards the rest
untouched, and each layer above speaks in the ordinary frames of the one
beneath, under a name that is its own. Any layer can be removed and the
ones beneath still stand; any layer can be added and the ones beneath do
not change. A new thing on the wire is assigned to its layer by a stated
rule that gives the same answer whoever applies it.

## The dimensions

- **Knowledge.** Whether any layer reads another's vocabulary — beneath it
  to act on something that is not its own, or above it to anticipate what
  will be built on it. The lowest layer is the one most tempted: a member it
  forwards for a layer above is a member it should not have.
- **The rule.** Whether every member and every name on the wire can be
  assigned to exactly one layer by a test a reader can apply — and whether
  the test is stated, so that the next question is answered by reading it
  rather than by repeating the afternoon that produced it.
- **Removability.** Whether a consumer can use the layers beneath a given
  one with the given one absent, and whether the ones beneath carry
  anything that exists only for the one above.
- **Additivity.** Whether a layer joins by adding — its own vocabulary, its
  own frames, its own events — with the ones beneath unchanged, or whether
  joining meant editing what was there.
- **Uniformity.** Whether a layer's own vocabulary is carried as the
  ordinary frames of the layer beneath — correlated, cancelled, dispatched
  like any other — or as a special case the layer beneath had to learn.
- **Boundaries between layers.** Whether a layer's vocabulary ever crosses
  a boundary it was not defined for — produced by the wrong side, forwarded
  where it should have been consumed, recorded as if it were the layer
  above's.

## What it yields to

The boundary: a layer's vocabulary is still mechanism, and a layer that
would carry a consumer's concept is the consumer's to build, outside. And
observability, in one respect: one observer across every layer means the
facts of a layer above travel through the layer beneath — resolved by the
layer above *telling through* the one beneath, which carries the fact and
reads nothing into it, so that the knowledge rule holds even where the
facts flow down.

## What it is not

Not a fixed number of layers: the count is whatever the domain needs, and
a layer that no consumer would use alone is not a layer. Not a hierarchy
where each layer wraps the one beneath in a format of its own: here every
layer's frames are the profile's frames, and layering is about who knows
what, not about encapsulation. Not an argument that fewer layers is better
— or more.
